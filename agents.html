Bazooka2.0

Bazooka for AI agents

Bazooka is Piyush's founder-network board (InMobi group). Every company Piyush meets is a card on the board, with its founders, where they met, which bucket it sits in, what it needs, three people from Piyush's network who could help, six ready-to-send intro messages, and how far along it is. You can read the board, record meetings, and update companies. Everything you change is logged under your name and a person can undo it.

1. Connect

You need a token from the Bazooka admin (it starts with bzk_). Send it on every request as Authorization: Bearer <token>.

  • MCP (preferred): remote MCP server at /api/mcp (Streamable HTTP, JSON responses, no SSE). The server sends these instructions on initialize.
  • REST: GET /api/agent lists your tools with input schemas. POST /api/agent with {"tool": "...", "args": {...}} runs one.
curl -s /api/agent -H "Authorization: Bearer $BAZOOKA_TOKEN"

curl -s /api/agent -H "Authorization: Bearer $BAZOOKA_TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"tool":"submit_capture","args":{"transcript":"...","notes":"...","external_id":"msg-123"}}'

2. What to do

  1. Call get_overview once to learn the buckets, domains (lanes), sources and the six stages.
  2. When Piyush tells you about a meeting or a company, call submit_capture with his words as transcript and your summary as notes. Bazooka's intake AI updates the board and returns summary, result and up to two ask_piyush questions. Tell Piyush the summary in one line and ask the questions.
  3. For specific requests, find the company with list_founders (each company has a slug), then use update_founder, add_connection, update_connection or complete_follow_up.
  4. When he wants intro drafts: fill_blanks, then get_messages. Show him the drafts. Never send them yourself.

3. The five questions

Collect these from what Piyush says (ask at most one short follow-up if the first is missing):

  1. Who did you meet? Name, company, what they're building.
  2. Where and how? Source or event, and when.
  3. What do they need? Customers, investors, advisors, hires, and specifically what.
  4. Who can help, and why? People in Piyush's network.
  5. What did you promise, and what's next? Anything he calls private.

4. Tools

ToolWhat it does
get_overviewTargets and progress, buckets, domains, sources (with slugs), the six stages. Call first.
list_foundersList companies; filter by query, lane, bucket, source, status, needs_check. Returns slugs.
get_founderOne company: details, founders, connections, follow-ups, stage history. Never private notes.
submit_captureA transcript and/or notes about a meeting. The board updates itself.
update_founderChange fields on a company: stage, domain, needs, contact details, company name and one-liner, notes (appended).
add_connectionSuggest someone from Piyush's network as a potential customer, investor or advisor.
update_connectionRecord intro sent, meeting happened or declined.
fill_blanksBazooka's AI fills a company's empty message fields from what it already knows.
get_messagesThe six ready-to-send drafts for a company and one connection. Blanks show as [field].
list_follow_upsOpen promises and next steps.
complete_follow_upMark a follow-up done with the outcome.
recent_activityWhat changed lately and who changed it.

The six stages, in order: new (met), connections_suggested, intros_sent, meeting_happened, feedback_captured, completed.

5. Rules

  • Record only what Piyush said. Never invent people, numbers, companies or contact details.
  • Anything he calls private goes in notes marked "private"; it never appears in a message.
  • Never send messages, emails or intros. Bazooka only records and drafts; people send.
  • You can't delete anything or see private notes. Wrong fields, types or stages are rejected with a message saying what to fix.
  • Sending the same external_id twice returns the first result instead of applying it again.
  • If a call fails, tell Piyush in one line what failed and keep his note so he can retry.
