Bazooka 2.0

Piyush records a conversation, or voice-types a short note when it wasn't recorded, into any of three places: the Grok bot, Notion AI or Instinct. Bazooka routes it everywhere it needs to go and comes back with the three most relevant connections for that founder. Notion Enterprise holds the context. This dashboard is the window into it.

    Capture kit

    Two pieces of text that make the pipeline work. Copy them into the bot or agent that needs them.

    Voice-note template

    For meetings that weren't recorded. Say it out loud, fill the brackets.

    Bazooka note. Founder: [full name], company [name], [one line on what they do]. Met via [source or event], on [date]. Domain: [bio-science, AI-native, space, defence, other]. Stage: [idea, pre-seed, seed, A], raising [amount] if any. Bucket: [Moonshot one, InMobi or Glance relevance and why; or ecosystem, just add value; or unlock and learning]. What they need: [first customers, investors, advisors, hires], specifically [detail]. Who I think can help: [names], because [reason]. I committed to: [what I promised, by when]. Conviction: [1 to 5]. Anything private: [say "private" and it stays out of every intro].

    Parser prompt

    System prompt for the Grok bot or the Notion Custom Agent.

    You are Bazooka, Piyush's founder-network chief of staff. From the note or transcript, extract JSON with: founder full_name, company, one_liner, source, source_event, met_on, domain, stage, raise, bucket ("moonshot_inmobi_glance" if relevant to InMobi/Glance business or the agentic-AI next S-curve; "unlock_learning" if the main value is helping the founder grow; otherwise "ecosystem") with bucket_reason, needs[], suggested_helpers[] with reasons, commitments[] with due dates, conviction 1–5, and private_notes. Never put private_notes in any outbound draft. If a field is missing, set it to null and list it under "ask_piyush" (at most 2 questions). Then rank the top 3 connections from the People database by fit to needs, closeness and stature, with one sentence of reasoning each. Draft intros using the message templates. Do not send anything. Queue the drafts for Piyush's daily approval digest.

    Roadmap

    From one HTML file to Grok as the main interface.

    1. Now

      This dashboard

      One HTML file with hardcoded data and copy-ready messages.

    2. By the 6th

      V1 working

      Real founder data, hosted on Vercel from a private GitHub repo, sending through one-tap WhatsApp and email links. Notion Founders and People databases set up with one Custom Agent that classifies and drafts.

    3. By the 9th

      Ready version

      Supabase behind login with no public reads, Notion to Supabase sync, follow-up scheduler, daily approval digest, and live funnel counters (50 intros to 25 meetings).

    4. Later

      Hands-free

      Automated WhatsApp sending through an official business number, Outlook automation once InMobi IT approves, and the Grok bot as the main interface.

    Data model

    The six tables Supabase will hold. Field names on this page already match them.

    sources

    Where Piyush meets founders, and how many each is expected to bring.

    id, name, full_name, expected, tentative

    founders

    One row per founder: company, lane, source, stage, needs, status and anything to verify.

    first_name, company, one_liner, lane, source_ids, status

    people

    Piyush's network: who they are, how close, how senior, what they can help with.

    first_name, title, org, tags, closeness, stature

    connections

    A suggested match between a founder and a person, with its type, reason, score and outcome.

    founder_id, person_id, type, reason, score, intro_sent

    message templates

    The six messages, with placeholders that fill from the other tables.

    key, title, channel, subject, body

    follow-ups

    The "how did it go?" check five days after each intro, and what came back.

    connection_id, due_on, reply, outcome
